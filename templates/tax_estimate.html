What this estimate covers

Income tax on employment and self-employment earnings, including CIS. Assumes the standard personal allowance before taper and subtracts eligible logged expenses and mileage from forecast earnings.

Excludes loss relief, capital allowances, trading allowance, pensions and other reliefs, savings, dividends, student loans, payments on account and tax already paid directly. The set-aside figure covers business income tax and standard Class 4 NI; it is not your complete Self Assessment bill.

Log actual income-tax deductions from payslips or contractor statements on each payment. Future PAYE and CIS deductions are never guessed. CIS deductions are advance credits and may differ from the final liability.
