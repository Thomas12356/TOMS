What this estimate covers

Income tax on employment and self-employment earnings, including CIS. Assumes the standard personal allowance before taper and treats gross business income as taxable earnings.

Excludes National Insurance, expenses and write-offs, trading allowance, pensions and other reliefs, savings, dividends, student loans, payments on account and tax already paid directly. The set-aside figure covers income tax only, not your entire Self Assessment bill.

PAYE estimates are planning assumptions, not payroll calculations. Enter expected deductions from payslips or HMRC for more accuracy. CIS deductions are advance credits and may differ from the final liability.
