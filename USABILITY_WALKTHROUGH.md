# Owner usability walkthrough

Use sample mode for this walkthrough so practice edits stay in sample records.
Login/password settings remain real. This is a guide for observing actual use;
the automated and expert checks do not measure owner hesitation or comprehension.

1. From Home, find the next payment needing attention. Follow guided review,
   check its classification and details, then confirm. Can you distinguish a
   suggested choice, saved evidence and confirmation without help?
2. Add a named income source using the short setup. Open its workspace and finish
   its forecast or choose individual shifts. Is it clear why an unfinished forecast
   remains unknown, and how work and bank receipts differ?
3. From a configured source, add work and return to its workspace. Reuse saved
   work/journey defaults, entering fresh dates, miles and reimbursement and checking
   eligibility. Open history and filter it. Can you find an older record and tell
   that filtering does not change the full recorded total or mileage relief?
4. Open Account money. Compare saved earmarks and the planning target. Request
   sample balances explicitly. Is it clear which values are saved, which are
   refreshed, and why earmarks do not prove funded or available spending cash?
5. Open Work and payment matching. Follow an unresolved invoice or receipt,
   review its evidence and make an explicit sample allocation where applicable.
   Inspect source totals. Can you tell why equal totals alone do not prove each
   shift has been paid?

For each task, note the first unclear label, unexpected destination, repeated
entry or explanation you needed. Also record whether you completed it independently.
Use those observations to choose the next refinement before adding presets,
broader automatic rules or budgeting. No owner observation results are recorded yet.
